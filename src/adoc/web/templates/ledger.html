What a deep review is

A deep review is a blind re-differential panel: several independent models each work up a fresh differential from your case file without seeing a-doc's current ledger, so they can't simply agree with what's already there. Anywhere their fresh take diverges from the existing picture gets adjudicated with an explicit, recorded rationale — a genuine second (and third) opinion, built into the product rather than left to chance.
